Good to know 05

Questions, answered.

Still wondering about something? Email us directly.

How much does a website cost?+

Every project is scoped around its goals, content and functionality. After a short discovery call, you’ll receive a clear proposal with deliverables, timing and cost — no hidden surprises.

How long does a project take?+

A focused business website typically takes four to eight weeks. Larger e-commerce or custom development projects may take longer. We’ll agree a realistic schedule before work begins.

Can you redesign my current website?+

Absolutely. We can audit what is working, preserve any valuable content or search equity, and rebuild the experience around your current business goals.

What happens after launch?+

We provide a clean handover and can stay involved through maintenance, content support, analytics and ongoing improvements.
